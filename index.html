Symmetrical Composition

The 2 people in front of the screen looked at each other，forming a seemingly symmetrical composition.
屏幕前的两个人对望着，就形成了好像对称的构图

Overhead

This height and angle form a top view composition
这个高度与角度，形成了顶视构图

Leading Lines

As we can see that they are in order in the picture.
我们可以明显的看到图中的他们是有次序的

Low Angle

The angle is low, making people feel likethey are looking up.
她的角度偏低，让人有仰视的感觉
